I’m guessing everyone in this room has done the exact same thing recently. We play this guessing game in our kitchens every day. And the cost of losing that game is staggering."

[0:45 - 1:30] The Solution (The Product)
(Slide 3: Clean mockup of the Fresco app interface showing a scan result: 'Mango - 3 Days Left - Store in Fridge'.)

"So, I built the timer. Meet Fresco.

Fresco is an AI-powered digital sommelier for your kitchen. We’ve built a decoupled, mobile-first application that removes the guesswork from food storage.

Here is how the user experience works: You open the app. You point your camera at a piece of fruit. In under two seconds, Fresco tells you three things: What it is, exactly how many days you have left to consume it, and whether it belongs on the counter or in the fridge."

[1:30 - 2:30] The "Magic" (The Tech & The Secret Sauce)
(Slide 4: A simple 3-step graphic: Camera 📸 → AI Brain 🧠 → Verdict ✅)

"Behind that simple interface is a complex, multi-step pipeline.

Our architecture uses a Next.js frontend communicating with a Python FastAPI backend. When a user snaps a photo, our computer vision models—which are currently in their final training epochs using transfer learning—isolate the fruit and analyze visual cellular degradation.

But we don't stop at AI. We combine that visual data with a biological, kinetic decay algorithm. For example, five days on a warm counter is very different from fourteen days in a cold fridge. Fresco does the math for you.

We even built an 'Ethylene Compatibility Engine.' Did you know bananas release a gas that makes strawberries rot twice as fast? Fresco flags those toxic roommate situations and tells you what to separate."

[2:30 - 3:00] The Market & Business Model
(Slide 5: Three simple columns: 'TAM: $X Billion', 'Target: Millennials', 'Model: Freemium')

"The market for this is massive. We are targeting the millions of eco-conscious consumers who shop at premium grocers and treat food as a lifestyle.

Our business model is simple: A freemium app for basic scanning, and a $4.99/month premium tier that unlocks 'The Cellar'—a smart, automated inventory system that tracks your entire kitchen and sends push notifications before anything expires."
