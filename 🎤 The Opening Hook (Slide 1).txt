📱 The Solution & Core Features (Slide 2 & 3)
Transition smoothly into the product.

"That is why we built this. A tool that removes the guesswork from your kitchen. You don't type anything. You just point your camera at the fruit."

To make your solution sound like a mature, venture-backed startup, you must present a complete ecosystem. Here are the necessary features you should highlight alongside your timeline/reminder feature:

The AI Freshness Timeline: (Your core feature). "Instant visual analysis that tells you exactly how many days you have left."

Proactive Push Notifications: "Your phone alerts you before the expiration date, not after the fruit is already dead."

Dynamic Storage Instructions: "It tells you exactly where to put it. Because a mango lasts a lot longer in the crisper drawer than it does sitting on a hot kitchen counter."

Ethylene Conflict Alerts (The "Toxic Roommate" feature): "Did you know bananas release a gas that makes nearby fruits rot faster? Our app flags these conflicts and tells you what to separate."

The Rescue Protocol (New Idea for the Pitch): "If a fruit is on its last day, the app dynamically suggests 'rescue recipes'—like turning those dying bananas into banana bread or a smoothie."

📋 Your 5-Slide Pitch Deck Structure
Keep your slides incredibly minimal. Let your voice do the selling.

Slide 1: The Problem (The Hook)

Visual: A stark, high-quality image of a single bruised, overripe fruit on a dark background.

Text: "We buy. We forget. We throw away."

Slide 2: The Solution

Visual: The high-end phone mockup of your app showing the scan result (e.g., "Mango — 4 Days Left").

Text: "Point. Scan. Know."

Slide 3: How It Works (The Features)

Visual: Three minimal icons (Camera, Bell for reminders, Snowflake/Sun for storage).

Text: "Visual Estimation | Smart Reminders | Storage Optimization"

Slide 4: The Tech / The Magic

Visual: A simple flowchart showing the camera feeding into an AI model.

Text: "Powered by Computer Vision." (Explain verbally that you are utilizing edge-optimized AI to read the visual signs of cellular decay).

Slide 5: The Close

Visual: Your logo and a strong closing statement.

Text: "Better information. Less waste."

Script: "We can't change the supply chain today, but we can fix the fruit bowl sitting on your counter. Thank you."
